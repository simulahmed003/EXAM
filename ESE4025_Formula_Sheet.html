Exam unit traps

  • Kelvin, not °C, in every COP, ratio or Carnot formula.
  • 210 goes with kg/min and TR only. If m is in kg/s, work in kW and divide by 3.5 at the end.
  • kJ/min ÷ 60 = kW.
  • Psychrometric h and w are per kg of dry air: multiply by dry-air flow, not total flow.
  • Δw in kg/kg, not g/kg, when you multiply by 2501.
  • ppm × 10−6 before dividing in the CO2 formula.
  • Duct f in mm H2O per m; convert to Pa with × 9.81 only if asked.
  • Write the unit on every line of a numerical; examiners give marks for it.

Values follow the study pack. Where your class notes use a different constant (for example 2500 instead of 2501), use the class one.
