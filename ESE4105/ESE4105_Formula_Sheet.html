Exam unit traps

  • kg/min ÷ 60 and kg/h ÷ 3600 before anything else.
  • Bar × 105 = Pa in ρ = p/RT and in IP; leave bar only when it cancels in a ratio.
  • Kelvin in T1; never °C in a gas law.
  • Venturi: divide C2 by Cv in the energy equation, but use the actual C2 in ṁ = ρAC.
  • Four-stroke: n = N/2 power strokes per minute. Two-stroke: n = N.
  • BP and IP come out in W if every input is in SI units; divide by 1000 for kW.
  • Gas fuels: divide O2 by 0.21 (volume). Solid/liquid fuels: divide by 0.23 (mass).
  • Efficiency above 100 % means a units slip or bad data: check once, then say so in one line.
  • Write the unit on every line of a numerical; the examiner gives marks for it.
