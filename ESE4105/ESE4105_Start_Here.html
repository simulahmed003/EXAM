24-hour priority list

You answer 3 of 4 questions in each part, so each part has its own list. Study down both lists until the green pass line: about 8 h for Part A and 6 h for Part B, which leaves time for sleep and a last read of the one-shot notes. Alternate between parts (A block 1, B block 1, A block 2 …) so neither is left short. Chance: how often the topic came up, newer papers weigh more (2024 ×5, 2023 ×4, 2022 ×3, 2021 ×2, 2020 ×1). Covered: share of that part's past-paper marks you have studied once you finish the row.

Next if you have time: in Part B, the rope brake numerical (B2, 20 marks in 2024) and the Otto efficiency derivation (B7, 2024) sit below the pass line only because they appeared once, in the newest paper. Both are pure method marks and take under an hour together: they are the best extra hour. B11 (CI combustion stages) is worth reading with B13/B14, because the knock answers lean on it.
