Exam traps

  • Convert the test interval to years before multiplying by a per-year failure rate.
  • Remove absence before computing AFR.
  • Divide TWA by 8 even when the shift is 9 h with lunch away.
  • In list questions, write the list first as headings, then one line each.

Values follow the study pack and the course materials. Where your class notes use a different constant, use the class one.
