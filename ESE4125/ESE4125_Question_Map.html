A14

Causes of accidents: Heinrich's domino theory, 88-10-2 rule, man-man, man-machine and man-environment relationships

Must study Very likely · 100%Easy

Asked in 5 of 5 papers · 52 marks in total

Study these

  • "Accidents do not happen, they are caused": Heinrich found 88 % unsafe acts, 10 % unsafe conditions, 2 % unpreventable
  • Domino theory: ancestry/social environment → fault of person → unsafe act/condition → accident → injury; remove one domino (usually the unsafe act/condition) and the injury is prevented
  • Three relationships: man-man, man-machine, man-environment; if any is out of balance → unsafe act/condition → accident

A1

Basic definitions: hazard, risk, accident, occupational safety, health, safety management; the three parts of OSHEM

Must study Very likely · 100%Easy

Asked in 5 of 5 papers · 49 marks in total

Study these

  • Hazard = a condition or act that CAN cause harm (to people, property, environment) but has not yet done so; risk = probability of the harm happening together with how bad it would be; accident = the unplanned event that actually causes the harm
  • OSHEM has three parts: occupational safety management (stop injury and damage now), occupational health management (stop slow illness), environment management (stop pollution of air, water, soil)
  • Safety management = systematic process of identifying, assessing and controlling risks so that accidents, injuries and illnesses are prevented

Must study Very likely · 87%Medium

A11

Anatomy of a process incident; event tree vs fault tree

Must study Likely · 67%Medium

Asked in 3 of 5 papers · 31 marks in total

Study these

  • A process drifts from normal → abnormal → emergency; each mode has its plant state, operating goal, critical systems and support activities
  • Normal: optimise, keep normal. Abnormal: return to normal, establish control. Emergency: bring to safe state, minimise losses
  • Fault tree works backward from a top event (deduction, AND/OR gates); event tree works forward from an initiating event (induction, success/failure branches) - they are logical counterparts

A8

BLEVE (boiling liquid expanding vapour explosion): mechanism, sequence of events, effects, prevention

Must study Likely · 67%Medium

Asked in 3 of 5 papers · 28 marks in total

Study these

  • A tank of liquefied gas (LPG) is heated by fire; pressure rises, wall above the liquid overheats and weakens, tank splits
  • Sudden pressure drop makes the superheated liquid flash-boil and expand ~250 times → blast + fireball + flying fragments
  • Prevent: cool the tank with water spray, insulation, relief valve, depressurise, slope/drain spills away, keep distance

A6

Fire basics: fire triangle and tetrahedron, factors contributing to fire, methods of extinguishment, types of explosion

Must study Possible · 53%Easy

Asked in 3 of 5 papers · 34 marks in total

Study these

  • Fire = rapid oxidation that gives heat and light; needs fuel + oxygen + heat (triangle) + chain reaction (tetrahedron)
  • Extinguish by removing one side: starving (remove fuel), smothering (cut oxygen), cooling/quenching (remove heat), inhibition (break chain reaction)
