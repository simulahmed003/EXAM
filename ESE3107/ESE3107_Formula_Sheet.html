Exam traps

  • Morning hour angles are negative.
  • γ is 0 facing south; west of south is positive.
  • Divide MJ/m² per hour by 3600 for W/m².
  • Tank losses use the start-of-hour temperature.

Values follow the study pack and the course materials. Where your class notes use a different constant, use the class one.
