Pass plan

Five papers (2019-2023). Normal papers: 210 marks, answer any THREE of four questions in each section (35 marks each). 2020 was a short exam (120 marks, any TWO of three per section, 30 marks each). Section A (Q1-Q4) = the sun and its radiation (angles, solar time, instruments, tilted surfaces), radiation heat transfer (laws, view factors, networks) and collector hardware (flat plate, evacuated tube). Section B (Q5-Q8) = flat-plate performance (losses, UL, F′, FR), concentrating collectors, water heating and storage, and other applications (drying, stills, cooling, ponds, power). Flat plate collector performance: losses, UL, F′, FR, critical radiation level, testing, numericals

Next to add Very likely · 100%Hard

Asked in 5 of 5 papers · 184 marks in total

Study these

  • Energy balance Qu = Ac[S − UL(Tp − Ta)] = AcFR[S − UL(Ti − Ta)]; S = (τα)IT; η = Qu/(AcIT)
  • Losses: top (convection + radiation through glazing), bottom (conduction through insulation), edge; UL = Ut + Ub + Ue; thermal network

  • Critical radiation level Ic = UL(Ti − Ta)/(τα); effective (τα)e ≈ 1.01-1.02τα; optimum tilt ≈ latitude
