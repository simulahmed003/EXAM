24-hour priority list

You answer 3 of 4 questions in each part, so each part has its own list. Study down both lists until the green pass line: about 2 h for Part A and 3 h for Part B, which leaves time for sleep and a last read of the one-shot notes. Alternate between parts (A block 1, B block 1, A block 2 …) so neither is left short. Chance: how often the topic came up, newer papers weigh more (2023 ×5, 2022 ×4, 2021 ×3, 2020 ×2, 2019 ×1). Covered: share of that part's past-paper marks you have studied once you finish the row.

Retake tip: read the Concept Primer first (30 minutes). Most marks here are theory you can learn by heart: collectors (A5), radiation laws (A3), instruments and radiation components (A2), concentrators (B2) and applications (B4). For numericals, the sun-angle calculator covers A1 and the storage-tank and still calculators cover B3 and B4. FPC performance (B1) and radiation networks (A4) are the hardest: leave them for last.
