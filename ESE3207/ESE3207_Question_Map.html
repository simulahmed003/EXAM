Silicon wafer and c-Si cell production: quartzite to wafer, cell fabrication, lithography, TCO, supporting layers

Pass set Very likely · 100%Easy

Asked in 5 of 5 papers · 136 marks in total

Study these

  • Quartzite (SiO2) + carbon in an arc furnace → metallurgical Si (98-99%) → HCl → trichlorosilane → distillation → Siemens CVD → polysilicon (9N+) → Czochralski / float-zone ingot → wire saw → etch, polish

  • TCO: transparent (Eg > 3 eV) and conductive oxide (ITO, SnO2:F, ZnO:Al) used as front electrode on thin-film cells; supporting layers (substrate/superstrate, buffer, window, ETL/HTL, back reflector)

A1

Semiconductor basics: bands, doping, Fermi level, direct/indirect gap, drift and diffusion, Poisson and continuity equations

Pass set Very likely · 100%Medium

Asked in 5 of 5 papers · 99 marks in total

Study these

  • Band model: valence band (bound electrons), conduction band (free electrons), bandgap Eg (Si 1.12 eV); a broken bond = free electron + hole
  • Doping: group V donor (P) → n-type, EF near EC; group III acceptor (B) → p-type, EF near EV; n = ND, p = ni2/ND

  • Transport: drift J = q(nμn + pμp)E and diffusion J = qD dn/dx; Einstein D/μ = kT/q; Poisson d2ψ/dx2 = −ρ/ε; continuity ∂n/∂t = (1/q)∂Jn/∂x + G − R

A2

Generation and recombination: radiative, SRH, Auger, surface; diffusion length; photocurrent numerical

Pass set Very likely · 87%Medium

Asked in 4 of 5 papers · 66 marks in total

Study these

  • Generation: a photon with E ≥ Eg lifts an electron to the CB (electron-hole pair); recombination is the reverse; in equilibrium G = R and np = ni2
  • Radiative (band-to-band, gives a photon; direct-gap materials), SRH (via a trap level from defects/impurities; dominant in Si), Auger (three carriers, energy to a third carrier; high doping), surface (dangling bonds)
  • Minority-carrier diffusion length L = √(Dτ): average distance a minority carrier travels before recombining; must exceed the absorber thickness
  • Lambert-Beer: Φ(x) = (1−R)Φ0e−αx; photon flux Φ0 = Pλ/(hc); Jph = q × absorbed flux

A3

Junctions: p-n junction solar cell, p-i-n, metal-semiconductor, diode equation; junction numericals

Pass set Very likely · 100%Hard

Asked in 5 of 5 papers · 76 marks in total

Study these

  • p-n junction: electrons and holes diffuse across, leaving fixed ions (depletion region) and a built-in field; light-made pairs reaching the field are separated: e− to n, h+ to p
  • Vbi = (kT/q) ln(NAND/ni2); W = √[(2ε/q)(Vbi ± V)(1/NA + 1/ND)]; Emax = √[(2q/ε)VbiNAND/(NA+ND)]

  • Metal-semiconductor: Schottky (rectifying) if φm > φs for n-type; ohmic if φm < φs (reversed for p-type); diode I = I0(eqV/kT − 1)

B2

Shading and mismatch; hot spots; bypass and blocking diodes

Pass set Very likely · 93%Easy

Asked in 4 of 5 papers · 53 marks in total

Study these

  • Shading: static (buildings, trees) and dynamic (clouds, dust, snow); in a series string the shaded cell limits the current of the whole string
  • Hot spot: shaded cell is driven into reverse bias and dissipates the power of the good cells as heat
  • Bypass diode: in parallel with a cell group, reverse polarity; conducts only when its group is shaded; I-V becomes a staircase with several power peaks
  • Blocking diode: in series between array and battery; stops the battery discharging into the array at night (and protects parallel strings)
  • Mismatch: cells/modules with different Isc, Voc (manufacturing tolerance, shading, soiling, temperature, ageing, orientation); fixes: sorting/binning, bypass diodes, string inverters, module-level MPPT (optimisers, micro-inverters)

B3

Battery, charge controller (set points, PWM, shunt and series regulators), MPPT (P&O, incremental conductance), DC-DC converters

Pass set Very likely · 87%Medium

Study these

  • Charge controller: prevents overcharge and deep discharge, blocks reverse current, load control, low-voltage disconnect
  • Set points: VR (regulation), VRH (regulation hysteresis), LVD (low-voltage disconnect), LVDH (LVD hysteresis); basic parameters: nominal system voltage, load current, PV array current

B4

Inverters: types by waveform and by mode, THD, converter efficiency and inverter selection numericals

Pass set Possible · 47%Easy

Asked in 2 of 5 papers · 24 marks in total

Study these

  • Inverter converts DC to AC; functions: power conversion, voltage regulation, frequency control (+ MPPT, synchronisation, anti-islanding for grid-tied)
  • By waveform: square (cheap, THD ~48%), modified sine (stepped, ~25-30%), pure sine (THD < 3-5%); by mode: standalone, grid-tied, bimodal/hybrid
