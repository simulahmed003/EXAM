24-hour priority list

You answer 3 of 4 questions in each part, so each part has its own list. Study down both lists until the green pass line: about 4 h for Part A and 2 h for Part B, which leaves time for sleep and a last read of the one-shot notes. Alternate between parts (A block 1, B block 1, A block 2 …) so neither is left short. Chance: how often the topic came up, newer papers weigh more (2023 ×5, 2022 ×4, 2021 ×3, 2020 ×2, 2019 ×1). Covered: share of that part's past-paper marks you have studied once you finish the row.

Retake tip: read the Concept Primer first (30 minutes). Part B is the easier half: on-grid/off-grid and net metering (B5), temperature and irradiance effects (B1), bypass diodes and shading (B2) and the solar home system sizing numerical (B6) come every year. In Part A, learn the I-V curve, FF and efficiency (A4, with a calculator), losses and the S-Q limit (A5) and quartzite-to-wafer (A6). Junction physics numericals (A3) are the hardest: use the calculator to practise them last.
