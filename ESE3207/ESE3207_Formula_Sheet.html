Exam traps

  • Convert cm² to m² before dividing into STC power.
  • Series adds voltage, parallel adds current.
  • Round PV, battery and inverter sizes up.
  • Octane-style mix-up to avoid: FF is a ratio, not a percentage of area.

Values follow the study pack and the course materials. Where your class notes use a different constant, use the class one.
