24-hour priority list

You answer 3 of 4 questions in each part, so each part has its own list. Study down both lists until the green pass line: about 3 h for Part A and 3 h for Part B, which leaves time for sleep and a last read of the one-shot notes. Alternate between parts (A block 1, B block 1, A block 2 …) so neither is left short. Chance: how often the topic came up, newer papers weigh more (2023 ×5, 2022 ×4, 2021 ×3, 2020 ×2, 2019 ×1). Covered: share of that part's past-paper marks you have studied once you finish the row.

Retake tip: read the Concept Primer first (30 minutes). Part A is mostly pumps and flow meters: the venturi/Pitot numericals (A4), affinity laws (A7) and the reciprocating pump numerical (A8) are short and come every year, and each has a calculator. In Part B the heat exchanger numericals (B5, B6) are the safest marks; the normal-shock calculator (B3) gives every downstream property in one go. Leave dimensional analysis (A3) and nozzle derivations (B2) for last.
