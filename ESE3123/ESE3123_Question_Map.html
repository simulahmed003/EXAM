Pass plan

Five papers (2019-2023). Normal papers: 210 marks, answer any THREE of four questions in each section (35 marks each). 2020 was a short exam (120 marks, any TWO of three per section). In 2020-2022 Section A = pipe flow, dimensional analysis, flow measurement and pumps; Section B = compressible flow and heat exchangers. The topics move: in 2023 Section A holds compressible flow, compressors and pumps while Section B holds measurement, pipe flow and heat exchangers; in 2019 Section A holds pipe flow, measurement and heat exchangers and Section B holds compressible flow, compressors and pumps. So this pack sorts by topic, not by question number. Minimum pass set (by topic): pipe flow (A1-A2), flow meters (A4), centrifugal pumps (A5-A7), reciprocating pumps (A8), compressible flow and nozzles (B1-B2), heat exchangers (B4-B6). In the paper this means: 2021/2022 A: Q1, Q2, Q3 or Q4; B: Q5, Q7, Q8. 2023 A: Q1, Q3, Q4; B: Q5 or Q6, Q7, Q8. 2019 A: Q1 or Q2, Q3, Q4; B: Q5, Q7, Q8. Answers follow the teachers’ material (Fahim sir: pipe flow, dimensional analysis, flow measurement, heat exchanger lectures; Shameem sir: centrifugal and reciprocating pumps, blowers and compressors) and the textbooks they use (Cengel & Cimbala, Bansal, Incropera, Holman). A6

Pump performance: characteristic curves, affinity laws, specific speed, cavitation and NPSH, priming, suction height

Pass set Very likely · 100%Easy

Asked in 5 of 5 papers · 112 marks in total

Study these

  • Main characteristics (H, P, η vs Q at several speeds), operating characteristics (H, P, η vs Q at the design speed), constant-efficiency (iso-η) curves; operating point = pump curve ∩ system curve
  • Affinity laws: Q ∝ ND3, H ∝ N2D2, P ∝ N3D5 (same pump: Q ∝ N, H ∝ N2, P ∝ N3)
  • Specific speed Ns = N√Q/Hm3/4: speed of a geometrically similar pump giving 1 m3/s against 1 m; fixes the impeller type
  • Cavitation: local p < vapour pressure ⇒ bubbles collapse ⇒ pitting, noise, fall in H and η; avoid with NPSHA > NPSHR; NPSH = ps/ρg + Vs2/2g − pv/ρg
  • Priming: fill suction pipe and casing with liquid (head ∝ ρ, air gives ~1/800 of the head); max suction height hs = patm/ρg − pv/ρg − hfs − Vs2/2g − NPSH

Reciprocating and other positive-displacement pumps: working, slip, classification, plunger vs diaphragm, numericals

Pass set Very likely · 100%Easy

Asked in 5 of 5 papers · 95 marks in total

Study these

  • Piston/plunger in a cylinder driven by crank and connecting rod; suction and delivery non-return valves; flow is pulsating
  • Qth = ALN/60 (single acting), 2ALN/60 (double acting, rod neglected), × number of cylinders for multi-throw

A1

Pipe flow theory: laminar and turbulent flow, Reynolds number, entrance region, losses, Darcy-Weisbach, laminar velocity profile

Pass set Very likely · 100%Medium

Asked in 5 of 5 papers · 109 marks in total

Study these

  • Reynolds number Re = ρVD/μ = inertia/viscous force; laminar Re < 2300, transitional 2300-4000, turbulent > 4000; hydraulic diameter Dh = 4Ac/p
  • Entrance region: boundary layer grows from the wall, irrotational core shrinks; fully developed when the profile stops changing (Lh ≈ 0.05ReD laminar, ≈ 10D turbulent)
  • Major loss hf = f(L/D)(V2/2g) (Darcy-Weisbach); minor losses hm = K V2/2g (entrance, exit, bends, valves, sudden enlargement/contraction)
  • Laminar: u(r) = 2Vavg(1 − r2/R2), umax = 2Vavg, ΔP = 32μLV/D2, f = 64/Re; Bernoulli from Euler’s equation

Next to add Very likely · 87%Hard

B3

Shock waves: normal and oblique shocks, Rayleigh line, normal-shock numericals

Next to add Very likely · 93%Medium

Asked in 4 of 5 papers · 86 marks in total

Study these

  • Shock wave: very thin (~0.1 μm) region where a supersonic flow suddenly becomes subsonic (normal) or turns (oblique); irreversible, entropy rises, P0 falls, T0 constant
  • Normal shock relations: Ma22 = (Ma12 + 2/(k − 1))/(2kMa12/(k − 1) − 1); P2/P1 = (1 + kMa12)/(1 + kMa22); T2/T1 = (2 + (k − 1)Ma12)/(2 + (k − 1)Ma22)
  • Rayleigh line: states with the same mass flux and momentum (continuity + momentum); Fanno line: same mass flux and energy; a normal shock jumps between their intersections

B6

Effectiveness-NTU method

Pass set Very likely · 93%Medium

Asked in 4 of 5 papers · 79 marks in total

Study these

  • Effectiveness ε = Q/Qmax, Qmax = Cmin(Th,i − Tc,i); NTU = UA/Cmin; Cr = Cmin/Cmax
  • Parallel: ε = [1 − e−NTU(1+Cr)]/(1 + Cr); counter: ε = [1 − e−NTU(1−Cr)]/[1 − Cre−NTU(1−Cr)]
