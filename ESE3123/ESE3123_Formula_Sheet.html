Exam traps

  • Check Re before using f = 64/Re.
  • Manometer: subtract 1 from the specific gravity ratio.
  • Round pump stages up.
  • Use kelvin and absolute pressure in compressible flow.
  • Counter-flow ΔT pairs are crossed: hot in with cold out.

Values follow the study pack and the course materials. Where your class notes use a different constant, use the class one.
