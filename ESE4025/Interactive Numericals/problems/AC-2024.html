7. Summer air conditioning with ventilation air and bypass factor: 2024 5(c)

Topic B3 · 15 marks · back to all numericals

A. Problem

A refrigeration (air-conditioning) plant is shown in the figure: room sensible heat 20 kW, room latent heat 5 kW, room 25 °C DBT / 50% RH, outside air 43 °C DBT / 27.5 °C WBT, return : outside air = 3 : 1 by mass, coil ADP 12 °C, bypass factor 0.13. Determine: (i) dehumidified air quantity, (ii) sensible and latent heat gain due to outside air, (iii) total refrigeration load of the plant.

B. UNDERSTANDHow the cycle/process works

For understanding – do not write

Path of the air (follow it on the chart with Step through): outside air o mixes with room return air i → mixture 1 on the straight line o–i, 1/4 of the way from i (3:1 by mass) → cooling coil pulls it toward the ADP (apparatus dew point, coil surface temperature) but 13% of the air bypasses the coil, so it leaves at 2 → supply air 2 absorbs the room heat and becomes i.

Dehumidified air = the air that goes through the coil = supply air. It must carry away the room sensible heat: ma = RSH / [cp(ti − t2)]. cpm = 1.0216 kJ/kg K is humid specific heat (1.005 + 1.88 W); many books use 1.02.

Mixing by mass: t1 = 0.75 ti + 0.25 to, W1 = 0.75 Wi + 0.25 Wo, h1 = 0.75 hi + 0.25 ho

Coil exit: t2 = tADP + X(t1 − tADP) = 12 + 0.13(29.5 − 12) = 14.28 °C

(i) Dehumidified air, ma = RSH / [cpm(ti − t2)] = 20 / [1.0216(25 − 14.28)] = 1.83 kg/s

Outside air, mo = 0.25 × 1.83 = 0.456 kg/s

(ii) OASH = mo cpm(to − ti) = 0.456 × 1.0216 × 18 = 8.39 kW

OALH = mo hfg(Wo − Wi) = 0.456 × 2501 × (16.8 − 9.9)/1000 = 7.88 kW

(iii) OATH = mo(ho − hi) = 0.456(86.6 − 50.3) = 16.57 kW

Total load = RSH + RLH + OATH = 20 + 5 + 16.57 = 41.6 kW = 11.8 TR

Chart sketch: mark o, i, join; 1 on it; ADP on saturation curve; line 1–ADP with 2 on it; line 2–i.

G. UNDERSTANDCommon mistakes

For understanding – do not write
  • Mixing by temperature only. Mix W and h by mass; t1 by mass is fine as an approximation.
  • Taking supply air = ADP. With X = 0.13 the air leaves above ADP: t2 = ADP + X(t1 − ADP).
  • Forgetting the outside-air load. The plant must handle room load + outside air load (GTH = RTH + OATH).
  • Chart reading. Readings differ by 2–3% between charts (the older pack used Wo = 17 g/kg and got 41.8 kW). The method gets the marks.
