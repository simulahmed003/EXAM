13. Duct sizing by equal friction: 2023 8(d)

Topic B9 · 15 marks · back to all numericals

A. Problem

Determine the size of the duct system shown in the figure using the equal friction method, assuming a pressure drop of 0.1 mm H2O/m length.

B. UNDERSTANDHow the cycle/process works

For understanding – do not write

Equal friction method: every section is sized for the same pressure loss per metre, f. So on the friction chart every section sits on one vertical line (f), at its own airflow Q; read the diameter there.

Step 1 is always the flows: start at the far outlets and add backwards toward the fan. Step 2 D from the chart or formula. Step 3 (if rectangular) b = 0.548 D, a = 3b for 3:1. Step 4 total friction = f × length of the longest run.

No aspect ratio is given, so round ducts are the answer; the rectangular 3:1 sizes are an optional extra (tick “rectangular” below).

G. UNDERSTANDCommon mistakes

For understanding – do not write
  • Wrong flows. A section carries the sum of all outlets downstream of it, not the outlet next to it.
  • Rectangle from area. Do not set ab = πD²/4; use the equivalent-diameter formula (same friction, not same area).
  • Units. f in mm H2O per m, Q in m³/s, D in m.
