3. Ammonia ice plant, dry saturated after compression: 2023 2(c)

Topic A11 · 12 marks · back to all numericals

A. Problem

An ammonia refrigerator produces 25 tonnes of ice from and at 0 °C in 24 hours. The temperature range of the compressor is from 15 °C to −5 °C. The vapour is dry saturated at the end of compression and an expansion valve is used. Assume a COP of 45% of the theoretical value. Calculate the power required to drive the compressor. Latent heat of ice is 335 kJ/kg.

B. UNDERSTANDHow the cycle/process works

For understanding – do not write

“Dry saturated at the end of compression” fixes point 2, not point 1: point 2 sits on the saturated-vapour curve at the condenser temperature. Compression is isentropic, so point 1 has the same entropy, which puts it inside the dome (wet vapour) at the evaporator temperature. That is why you find a dryness fraction x1 from entropy first.

Ice load: making ice “from and at 0 °C” needs only the latent heat (335 kJ/kg); there is no sensible part. Divide by 86 400 s to get kW.

Actual COP = given percentage × theoretical COP. Power = load / actual COP. (The interactive graph shows the theoretical power m w; divide it by the percentage to get the real answer.)

Interpolation here: Table A-13 prints 12 and 16 °C (15 °C is 3/4 of the way: fraction 0.75) and −6 and −4 °C (−5 °C is the midpoint).

F. WRITE IN EXAMModel answer

Write this in the exam

Given: NH3, 25 t of ice per day from and at 0 °C, tc = 15 °C, te = -5 °C, dry saturated after compression, COP = 45% of theoretical, L = 335 kJ/kg.

Property (Table A-13)at 15 °Cat -5 °C
hf (kJ/kg)250.44156.86
hg (kJ/kg)1456.041436.42
sf, sg (kJ/kg K)sg = 5.14330.6248, 5.3967
15 °C = 12 + 0.75(16 − 12): interpolated between the 12 and 16 °C rows. −5 °C = average of the −6 and −4 °C rows.

State 2 is dry saturated at 15 °C: h2 = hg = 1456.04 kJ/kg, s2 = 5.1433

1→2 isentropic: s1 = s2 ⇒ x1 = (s2 − sf1)/(sg1 − sf1) = (5.1433 − 0.6248)/(5.3967 − 0.6248) = 0.9469

h1 = hf1 + x1(hg1 − hf1) = 156.86 + 0.9469(1436.42 − 156.86) = 1368.48 kJ/kg

h4 = h3 = hf(15 °C) = 250.44 kJ/kg

COPth = (h1 − h4)/(h2 − h1) = (1368.48 − 250.44)/(1456.04 − 1368.48) = 12.77

COPactual = 0.45 × 12.77 = 5.746

Refrigeration load Q = (ice per day × L) / seconds per day = 25 × 1000 × 335 / (24 × 3600) = 96.93 kW

Power = Q / COPactual = 96.93 / 5.746 = 16.87 kW

Sketch (T–s or P–h): point 1 inside the dome (wet, x1 ≈ 0.95), 1→2 vertical on T–s ending on the saturated-vapour curve at 15 °C, 3 on the saturated-liquid curve, 3→4 constant h.

G. UNDERSTANDCommon mistakes

For understanding – do not write
  • Putting point 1 on the vapour curve. Here point 2 is dry saturated; point 1 is wet (x1 ≈ 0.95).
  • Adding sensible heat to the ice load. “From and at 0 °C” means latent heat only.
  • Applying 45% the wrong way. Actual COP is smaller than theoretical, so power is bigger: P = Q/(0.45 COPth).
  • Table zero. Other ammonia tables (e.g. the course slides) use a different zero and give h values about 1 kJ/kg different; COP and power come out the same to 3 figures (the old pack got 16.9 kW).
