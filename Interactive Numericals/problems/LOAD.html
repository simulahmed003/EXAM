10. Room cooling load with windows on two walls: 2024 7(c)

Topic B8 · 15 marks · back to all numericals

A. Problem

Two of the wall faces, N and W, contain double-glazed windows (see figure). There is no heat gain through other walls. Find the required cooling capacity. Outside 43 °C DBT, 24 °C WBT; U of wall, roof, floor 1.78, 1.3, 1.2 W/m²K; ETD wall 25 °C, roof 30 °C; SHG 300 W/m², SC 0.86; lights 33 W/m² floor; 4 occupants (90 W sensible + 40 W latent each); appliances 600 W sensible + 400 W latent; infiltration 0.5 ACH. (Inside condition printed as “43 °C, 55%”: misprint, taken as 25 °C / 50%.)

B. UNDERSTANDHow the cycle/process works

For understanding – do not write

Cooling load = sum of every heat gain into the room. Two families: sensible (raises temperature) and latent (adds moisture). Each line uses one simple formula: walls and roof U A ETD (ETD = equivalent temperature difference, includes sun on the wall), glass A(U ΔT + SHG × SC), infiltration m cp ΔT and m hfg ΔW, people and appliances as given, lights W/m² × floor area.

G. UNDERSTANDCommon mistakes

For understanding – do not write
  • Deducting the window from the wall area. Wall area is net of glass.
  • Using ETD for glass. Glass uses UΔT + SHG × SC.
  • State every assumption in one line at the top.
