Exam traps

  • The diffractometer reports 2θ: double your Bragg angle.
  • Zero counts as even in hkl rules.
  • In series the smaller capacitor takes the larger voltage.

Values follow the study pack and the course materials. Where your class notes use a different constant, use the class one.
