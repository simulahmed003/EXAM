Exam traps

  • Degrees mode for every tan / cos in shading numericals.
  • Kelvin in every T4 and in Swinbank.
  • Degree-days are in K·day: multiply by 24 to get hours before using watts.
  • U already includes both surface films if the question says so; do not add 1/h again.
  • Solar fraction uses Ta on top and Ti at the bottom.
  • Write the unit on every line.

Values follow the study pack and the course materials. Where your class notes use a different constant, use the class one.
