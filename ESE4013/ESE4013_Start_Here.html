24-hour priority list

You answer 3 of 4 questions in each part, so each part has its own list. Study down both lists until the green pass line: about 6 h for Part A and 6 h for Part B, which leaves time for sleep and a last read of the one-shot notes. Alternate between parts (A block 1, B block 1, A block 2 …) so neither is left short. Chance: how often the topic came up, newer papers weigh more (2024 ×4, 2022 ×3, 2021 ×2, 2020 ×1). Covered: share of that part's past-paper marks you have studied once you finish the row.

Next if you have time: the two numericals, overhang sizing (A12) and the sol-air wall heat gain (B4), are short and pure formula marks: learn them with the calculators even if they sit below the pass line. In Part B, B6 (transient heat flow) and B8 (degree-days) are long but come every year, so learn their diagrams and the one key equation each.
