Load curve, load duration curve and the plant factors (load, demand, diversity, capacity, use)

Must study Very likely · 93%Easy

Asked in 4 of 5 papers · 50 marks in total

Study these

  • Load curve = load vs time of day (chronological); load duration curve (LDC) = the same loads sorted from highest to lowest; area under either curve = energy
  • Load factor = average load / maximum demand; demand factor = maximum demand / connected load (≤ 1); diversity factor = sum of individual maximum demands / maximum demand of the whole system (≥ 1)
  • Capacity factor = average load / installed capacity; use factor = energy produced / (capacity × hours actually run)

Must study Very likely · 100%Medium

Asked in 5 of 5 papers · 62 marks in total

B5

Brayton cycle numericals and the maximum-work condition

Must study Likely · 67%Hard

Asked in 4 of 5 papers · 51 marks in total

Study these

  • T2/T1 = T3/T4 = rp(γ−1)/γ = rp0.286 for air (isentropic); actual: T2 = T1 + (T2s − T1)/ηc, T4 = T3 − ηt(T3 − T4s)
