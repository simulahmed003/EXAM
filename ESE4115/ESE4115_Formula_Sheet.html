Exam traps

  • Kelvin in every Brayton and chimney formula.
  • Divide by ηc for the compressor, multiply by ηt for the turbine.
  • Use capacity factor (not load factor) for energy when both are given.
  • Seconds in a year: 31.536 × 106.
  • Money: crore = 107, lac = 105.
  • If a heat rate gives an efficiency below 10 %, say it is unrealistic in one line and carry on.
