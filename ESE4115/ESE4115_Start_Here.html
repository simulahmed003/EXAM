24-hour priority list

You answer 3 of 4 questions in each part, so each part has its own list. Study down both lists until the green pass line: about 6 h for Part A and 6 h for Part B, which leaves time for sleep and a last read of the one-shot notes. Alternate between parts (A block 1, B block 1, A block 2 …) so neither is left short. Chance: how often the topic came up, newer papers weigh more (2024 ×5, 2023 ×4, 2022 ×3, 2021 ×2, 2020 ×1). Covered: share of that part's past-paper marks you have studied once you finish the row.

Next if you have time: the 2024 exergy numericals (B7, 25 marks each) appeared only once, so they sit low. They are long; learn them only after the pass line. The Brayton calculator (B5) and the hydro calculator (A14) let you redo every year's numbers in minutes, so practise those first: A3, A8 and A14 are numericals that come almost every year.
