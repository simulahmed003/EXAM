24-hour priority list

You answer 3 of 4 questions in each part, so each part has its own list. Study down both lists until the green pass line: about 6 h for Part A and 5 h for Part B, which leaves time for sleep and a last read of the one-shot notes. Alternate between parts (A block 1, B block 1, A block 2 …) so neither is left short. Chance: how often the topic came up, newer papers weigh more (2024 ×4, 2023 ×3, 2021 ×2, 2020 ×1). Covered: share of that part's past-paper marks you have studied once you finish the row.

Next if you have time: A4 in Part A (R22 80 kW numerical, asked word for word in 2021 and 2024) and B3 in Part B (summer AC with bypass factor, 15 marks in 2021 and 2024). Both sit just below the pass line but repeat almost unchanged, so they are the best extra hour in each part.

Pass plan

Each section has 4 questions of 35 marks and you answer 3 (2020 was a shorter 120-mark paper). Section A = refrigeration, Section B = air conditioning. "Must study" topics came up in at least 3 of the 4 papers, or are the same numerical repeated.

Section A: Refrigeration

8 topics

cover 64% of all Section A marks asked

  • VCR cycle, COP vs temperatures, TR and ice load
  • R22 numerical (same 80 kW question twice)
  • Multistage + flash cooler, refrigerants, hermetic compressor
  • Absorption: single, double, triple effect

Section B: Air conditioning

9 topics

cover 89% of all Section B marks asked

  • Psychrometric definitions (12 marks every year)
  • Summer AC + bypass factor numerical, comfort chart, IAQ
  • CAV / VAV / dual duct, VRF three-pipe, cooling load
  • Duct sizing numerical (every year, 12 to 15 marks)
  • Never skip a numerical. R22 cycle, summer AC with bypass factor, and duct sizing together are worth about 45 marks and repeat almost unchanged.
  • Definitions and sketches are cheap marks. Psychrometric terms, comfort chart, VRF three-pipe, flash cooler and double-effect VAR sketches show up again and again.
  • "Good to have" topics are your backup for the third question in a section. "Skip if short" appeared once or for few marks.

Ton of refrigeration, COP and ice / cold-storage load

Must study Very likely · 100%Easy

Asked in 4 of 4 papers · 43 marks in total

Study these

  • 1 TR = 3.517 kW (210 kJ/min): melting 1 ton of ice in 24 h
  • COP; refrigerator vs heat pump; refrigeration vs air conditioning
  • Load = m(cΔT + latent) / time, then divide by 3.517 for TR
  1. 2024Q1(a)10 marks

  2. 2023Q1(d)7 marks

  3. 2021Q1(a)8 marks

  5. 2020Q1(c)10 marks
