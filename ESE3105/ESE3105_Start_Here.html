24-hour priority list

You answer 3 of 4 questions in each part, so each part has its own list. Study down both lists until the green pass line: about 3 h for Part A and 4 h for Part B, which leaves time for sleep and a last read of the one-shot notes. Alternate between parts (A block 1, B block 1, A block 2 …) so neither is left short. Chance: how often the topic came up, newer papers weigh more (2023 ×5, 2022 ×4, 2021 ×3, 2020 ×2, 2019 ×1). Covered: share of that part's past-paper marks you have studied once you finish the row.

Retake tip: read the Concept Primer first (30 minutes). In Part A the wall/pipe resistance numerical (A2), critical radius (A3) and the fin derivation plus numerical (A4) come every year and all have calculators. The boiling curve (A6) is pure theory: learn the five regions. In Part B learn the dimensionless numbers (B1) and the flat-plate numerical (B3); natural convection on a plate (B6) is the easiest numerical. Tube banks (B5) are long: do them last.
