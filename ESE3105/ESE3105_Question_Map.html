Pass plan

Five papers (2019-2023). Normal papers: 210 marks, answer any THREE of four questions in each section (35 marks each). 2020 was a short exam (120 marks, any TWO of three per section, 30 marks each). Section A (Q1-Q4) = conduction (equation, walls/pipes, insulation, fins), transient conduction, boiling and condensation. Section B (Q5-Q8) = convection (boundary layers, flat plate, pipes, tube banks, natural convection) and mass transfer. Minimum pass set: Section A: Q1 (modes + conduction equation + wall/pipe numerical), Q2 (critical insulation + fins), Q4 (boiling curve + condensation + condenser numerical). Section B: Q5 (convection mechanism, boundary layers, flat-plate numerical), Q7 (tube bank numerical or natural-convection plate), Q8 (Fick’s law + a diffusion numerical). The course notes (Mahmudul sir, Mahfuz notes, Sohag sir) follow Cengel’s Heat and Mass Transfer, so answers use Cengel’s methods and table values; every numerical was re-checked in Python.
