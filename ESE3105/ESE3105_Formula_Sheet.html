Exam traps

  • Use radii in ln(r2/r1); per-metre answers are W/m.
  • Air properties at the film temperature (Ts + T∞)/2.
  • Check Bi first in every transient problem.
  • Horizontal plate: L = area/perimeter, not the side length.

Values follow the study pack and the course materials. Where your class notes use a different constant, use the class one.
