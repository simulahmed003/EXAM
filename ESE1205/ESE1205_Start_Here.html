24-hour priority list

You answer 3 of 4 questions in each part, so each part has its own list. Study down both lists until the green pass line: about 5 h for Part A and 4 h for Part B, which leaves time for sleep and a last read of the one-shot notes. Alternate between parts (A block 1, B block 1, A block 2 …) so neither is left short. Chance: how often the topic came up, newer papers weigh more (2023 ×7, 2022 ×6, 2021 ×5, 2020 ×4, 2019 ×3, 2018 ×2, 2017 ×1). Covered: share of that part's past-paper marks you have studied once you finish the row.

Retake tip: read the Concept Primer for each block first (30 minutes), then follow this list. Psychrometry (B8) and gas mixtures (B7) are the biggest mark-earners in Part B and both have calculators; the Otto/Diesel numerical (B4) comes every year. In Part A the first law (A5, A6) and the second law (A7) are the safest. Entropy and exergy (A8, A9) are harder: do them last, using the calculators to practise.
