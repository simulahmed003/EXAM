Pass plan

Seven papers (2017-2023). Normal papers: 210 marks, answer any THREE of four questions in each section (35 marks per question). 2020 was a short exam (120 marks, any TWO of three per section). Section A (Q1-Q4) = basic concepts, pure substance and gases, first law, second law, entropy, exergy, relations. Section B (Q5-Q8) = power cycles (Rankine, Otto, Diesel, Dual, Brayton), refrigeration, gas mixtures and psychrometry. Minimum pass set: the "Pass set" topics let you write three full questions in each section: in Section A, Q1 (concepts, pure substance), Q2 (first law + device numerical) and Q3 (second law + entropy); in Section B, one cycle question (Rankine or IC engine), Q7 (gas mixture) and Q8 (psychrometry). These are the most predictable questions on the paper and their numericals repeat almost word for word. Answers follow the course slides (Tahir Mahmud, Afsar/Sohag lectures, Cengel and Moran-Shapiro examples used in the slides); steam values are Cengel/Moran table values, checked with an IAPWS steam-property program.

A5

Zeroth and first law; energy balance for closed and open systems; boundary work

Pass set Very likely · 100%Medium

Asked in 7 of 7 papers · 123 marks in total

Study these

  • Zeroth law → temperature; first law → internal energy; second law → entropy

  • Control volume mass and energy rate balance; derivation for one inlet and one exit; steady-flow energy equation (SFEE)
  • Boundary work W = ∫p dV: constant pressure p(V2−V1), polytropic (p2V2−p1V1)/(1−n), isothermal p1V1ln(V2/V1)
