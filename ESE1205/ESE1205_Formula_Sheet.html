Exam traps

  • Kelvin everywhere except the psychrometric enthalpy (which uses °C).
  • Compressor work negative, turbine positive in the SFEE.
  • Check the paper for misprints (e.g. 50 MPa for 50 kPa) and say which you used.
  • Ideal turbine on the bottom of ηT, on top of ηC.

Values follow the study pack and the course materials. Where your class notes use a different constant, use the class one.
